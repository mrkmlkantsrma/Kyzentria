# Ads Strategy

Start without ads until Kyzentria has useful pages, search traffic, and repeat visitors.

Ad networks to consider:
- Google AdSense
- Ezoic
- Mediavine

When to add ads:
- After the site has real traffic
- After key pages have original content
- After navigation, privacy policy, contact page, and sitemap are ready
- After affiliate placements are tested

Where ads can work:
- Blog-style guides
- Comparison pages
- List pages
- Long SEO pages
- Tool result pages after the user finishes an action

Where ads should be avoided or limited:
- Main tool input areas
- Buttons
- Forms
- Conversion-focused affiliate tables
- Any place that makes the tool feel slow or confusing

Recommended layout:
- One ad after intro content on long pages
- One ad after the main tool
- One ad near the bottom of long guides

Do not overload early pages with ads. Useful tools build trust first; ads can come after the traffic proves itself.
